Marginal measured overhead of IL-guard relative to IL-no-guard for slot-level control-path time. Positive values indicate higher measured control-path cost, while negative values indicate lower measured control-path time under the corresponding workload and capacity. The green ring marks the lowest measured delta and the red ring marks the largest measured increase; neither marker is treated as a statistical optimum. Negative values are workload-dependent measured outcomes and are not interpreted as evidence that Guardrails is intrinsically cheaper. Values are descriptive over three repeated runs.
