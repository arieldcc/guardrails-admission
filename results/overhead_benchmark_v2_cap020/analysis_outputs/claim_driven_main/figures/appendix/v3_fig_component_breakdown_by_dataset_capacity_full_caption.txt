Appendix component-level attribution of measured admission-pipeline time across datasets and cache capacities. Stacked bars show component shares over measured components only. Numbers above bars report the corresponding mean total measured component time in seconds over three repeated runs. Guard-NoG labels report the paired mean total measured component-time difference between IL-guard and IL-no-guard for the same dataset and cache capacity. Bar labels denote NoG = IL-no-guard, Guard = IL-guard, and GBDT = Delayed-GBDT. The figure is descriptive and does not imply statistical significance from three runs.
